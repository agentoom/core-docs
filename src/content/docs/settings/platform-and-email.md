# Platform & Email Configuration

Agentoom sends security OTP codes, compliance alerts, and incident notifications through your configured email transport.

---

## ✉️ SMTP Configuration

In **Settings > Platform > Email Configuration**, enter your corporate mail server credentials:
- **Host & Port**: e.g., `smtp.mailgun.org`, port `587` (TLS).
- **Sender Address**: e.g., `security-alerts@yourcompany.com`.
- **Test Delivery**: Send a test verification email directly from the UI.
