# Resources and Knowledge Bases

Grounding your AI assistants in verified internal documentation prevents hallucinations and ensures accurate, up-to-date responses.

---

## 📚 Knowledge Base Ingestion

Agentoom supports seamless ingestion of business documents:
- **Supported Formats**: PDF, Markdown (`.md`), Word (`.docx`), Plain Text (`.txt`).
- **Automatic Chunking**: Documents are split into semantic paragraphs with overlap preservation.
- **Vector Search Engine**: Chunk embeddings are stored in high-performance local vector indices (Typesense / SQLite vector / PostgreSQL pgvector).
- **Provenance Tracking**: Every response generated by an agent includes citations and source document attribution, fulfilling transparency requirements under the EU AI Act.
